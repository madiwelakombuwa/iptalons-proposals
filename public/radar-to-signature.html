02 Where deals live today

A patchwork: Proposify, spreadsheets, calendar reminders.

Every IPTalons proposal is a fixed brand shell around two hand-written prose sections — the introduction letter in your voice and the savings analysis — plus arithmetic on researcher count. The bespoke part is the bottleneck, and it's exactly what generic tools can't do.

Proposify can't write the letter

It gives you templates. The intro letter — the highest-effort, most bespoke section of every proposal — is still written by hand, every time.

No memory of the relationship

Follow-ups live in calendars, notes in spreadsheets, pipeline in heads. Nothing connects a prospect's first signal to the proposal they eventually receive.

Pricing done by hand

Seats × $249, bundle discounts, the "Your Investment" table — assembled manually, so it can be fat-fingered. It never needs to be.

03 What already works

Two working tools, one at each end of the funnel.

Live · auto-refreshed daily

CSR Demand Radar

Finds the people already asking for CSR — by name, in their own words.

  • Daily sweep of r/SBIR, r/NIH and X, qualified down to people describing their own compliance pain — plus institutions hiring RSOs.
  • Every lead ships armed: source link, the prospect's own quote, and a CSR-specific opening angle.
  • Team pipeline already inside: statuses New → Won, owners, notes, a kanban board and an activity log — a CRM seed.
  • Value-first compose drafts, always edited and sent by a human.
Working demo

Proposal Tool

Generates the Baylor-format proposal, with Claude doing the bespoke parts.

  • 3-step wizard: Claude drafts the intro letter and savings analysis in your established voice — NSPM-33/CHIPS opening, credibility stats baked in.
  • Tier templates auto-price from researcher count; bundle discounts apply themselves.
  • 8-page print-ready preview, matched to the Baylor PDF — with the full product story built in: the 6-step CSR certification journey, platform features (Digital File Cabinet, risk interview, ORCID verification), and the included RedBook™ & Grant Hopper AI tools.
  • Proposal Assistant chat: tighten the letter, sharpen savings, handle pricing objections live.

The radar knows who and why now. The proposal tool knows what to say and what to charge. They've never met.

05 The CRM layer

Grow the radar's pipeline board into the CRM of record.

The radar already tracks status, owner, notes and activity per lead. The CRM layer promotes that into full account management — purpose-built for how IPTalons actually sells, not a Salesforce to configure.

Accounts & contacts

Radar leads, inbound inquiries and conference contacts in one place — institution, RSO / VP-Research contact, federally funded PI count, primary risk concern.

Follow-ups that surface themselves

Owner + next-follow-up date on every account, with urgency coloring and a follow-ups-due queue. Overdue items and signed proposals raise notifications.

An honest activity timeline

Every touch — radar signal, outreach sent, reply, proposal viewed, signed — on one timeline per account. Analytics computed from real history, not estimates.

07 Why build, not subscribe

A generic proposal SaaS has never heard of NSPM-33.

The bespoke part is what LLMs are best at

Proposify templates the shell; it can't write the letter. Claude drafts both prose sections in your established voice, grounded in the prospect's actual risk profile, in seconds.

A sales engineer, not a document editor

The assistant knows FIGs, Section 117, and why RedBook matters. It answers compliance questions and drafts objection-handling live, during the deal conversation.

One tool replaces the patchwork

Proposals + CRM + follow-ups + pipeline analytics + notifications in one system, instead of Proposify + spreadsheets + calendar reminders.

Cost, control, and a credibility story

No per-seat subscription. It runs on the same Cloudflare Workers stack as CSR itself, data stays in-house — and IPTalons already ships AI to customers. Dogfooding the platform is on-brand.

08 Roadmap

Three phases. The first is mostly assembly.

Phase 1Unify

Radar + proposal tool become one app

Promote radar leads into the proposal tool's prospect records; carry the CRM fields (owner, follow-up, activity) across; single login, single pipeline view. Wire the Settings model choice into the drafting calls and give the assistant conversation memory. Same Cloudflare stack we already run.

Phase 2Send & track

The flagship Proposify feature — done better

Shareable recipient links with open/view tracking ("your prospect viewed the proposal 3×"), real email sending, and notifications that fire from actual prospect behavior. Analytics computed from stored history; working exports.

Phase 3Close

Signature and structure

Real e-signature backend (the DocuSign-style flow exists in the UI today, simulated), section-level template editing so proposal structure isn't fixed, and a clean handoff of won deals into CSR onboarding.

These phases map one-to-one to the demo's known gaps — this is an honest inventory, not a wishlist. Everything in Phase 1 builds on code that is already running.
