# IPTalons Proposal Workspace — managed paid pilot

**Status:** commercial draft for internal approval. This is product packaging, not a signed customer agreement or legal advice.

## The offer

IPTalons receives one managed proposal-and-outreach workspace for its approved team. The workspace turns qualified signals into prospects, builds branded proposals, publishes controlled recipient versions, records engagement, and summarizes team activity in one shared system.

The pilot proves repeated business use and operating cost before building self-service billing or multi-company tenancy.

## Recommended pilot terms

- **Term:** 90 days.
- **Onboarding:** **$1,500 one time**, covering workspace setup, named-user access, initial import review, branding, sender-domain configuration, and a live team onboarding session.
- **Managed service:** **$499 per month**, billed monthly in advance.
- **Pilot total:** **$2,997** before taxes and any separately approved usage overages.
- **Included users:** four named administrators. Additional users require written approval during the pilot.
- **Included support:** up to two hours per month for training, configuration, and incident support. Larger workflow or product changes receive a separate estimate.
- **Renewal:** month-to-month after the pilot only after both parties review usage, support load, provider cost, and data requirements.

This price is deliberately above a self-service software price because the current offer includes setup, monitoring, and direct support. Discount the pilot only in exchange for something measurable, such as a documented case study, weekly feedback, or a longer prepaid term.

## Included product scope

- Named-user access for the approved IPTalons team.
- Shared prospects and proposals with conflict detection and immutable revision history.
- Apify-backed signal collection with human review state.
- Branded proposal drafting and pricing configuration.
- Immutable public proposal versions with high-entropy links, expiry, revocation, and request counts.
- Resend proposal delivery after the sender domain and API key are configured.
- Team activity summaries for prospects created, proposals produced, estimated proposal value, and recorded outcomes.
- Administrator-managed import/export and a documented recovery process.
- Staging validation before reviewed production releases.

## Explicit exclusions

- The workspace is not a research-records system, compliance certification platform, legal determination engine, or system of record for regulated research data.
- Electronic signatures are not connected. Signing stays in the customer’s separately approved signing service.
- Proposal views are page requests and may include security scanners or automated systems; they do not prove a named person read a proposal.
- Apify signals are sales-research inputs requiring human review. They do not establish identity, intent, eligibility, or compliance status.
- AI drafting remains disabled until provider credentials, spending limits, approved use, and output-review procedures are configured.
- Self-service signup, card checkout, automatic subscription changes, and multiple independent customer companies are outside this pilot.
- Formal uptime, response-time, security-certification, and data-residency commitments require separately reviewed terms and evidence.

## Customer responsibilities

- Approve named users and promptly request removal when access changes.
- Confirm that imported records, prospect information, and source data may be used for this sales workflow.
- Review all proposal text, pricing, claims, and recipient addresses before publication or sending.
- Keep regulated, export-controlled, health, student, credential, and research-security case data out of the workspace unless a later agreement explicitly covers it.
- Control the IPTalons DNS domain and Resend account used for outbound email.

## Provider responsibilities

- Operate isolated staging and production resources and release reviewed code through the documented process.
- Preserve customer-facing proposal snapshots independently from later draft edits.
- Restrict workspace access to approved identities and retain a revocable membership record.
- Maintain reasonable backups, exercise restoration, and document material incidents.
- Keep API credentials in Cloudflare secrets and avoid placing proposal bodies, access tokens, or recipient links in routine logs.
- Disclose material product limitations and provider incidents that affect the service.

## Pilot success measures

Review these at days 30, 60, and 90:

- Approved team members who used the workspace weekly.
- Qualified signals reviewed and converted to prospects.
- Proposals created, published, and sent.
- Time from qualified signal to first proposal.
- Recipient requests, replies, meetings, and manually confirmed deals.
- Estimated and closed deal value, with human-confirmed outcome definitions.
- Support hours, provider costs, error rate, and recovery incidents.
- Data or workflow gaps that required work outside the product.

## Launch conditions

The paid pilot starts only after:

1. The contracting entity, invoice recipient, tax treatment, and payment method are confirmed.
2. The customer agreement, privacy terms, data-retention period, and support contact are approved.
3. Resend sender-domain verification and controlled deliverability tests pass.
4. Final product and proposal copy is approved by IPTalons.
5. A fresh staging acceptance test passes for the production candidate.
6. Production resources are backed up and the exact release commit and Worker version are recorded.

## Deferred pricing decision

Do not add public checkout for this offer. Issue an invoice through the approved contracting entity and grant access only after payment is verified. Record the pilot start and end dates administratively. Build subscription billing only when the product is ready to serve independent companies with enforced tenant isolation.
